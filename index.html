theseuspress.com ↗

Second Year Seniors

secondyearseniors.com ↗

America has two demographic crises converging at once: colleges running out of students, and a generation running out of good places to grow old. Second Year Seniors argues these aren't separate problems — they're each other's solution. Struggling campuses, reimagined as communities built for the Peak 65 generation, could give older adults the connection, purpose, and vitality they're looking for, and give colleges a reason to exist. Everyone deserves a senior year.

Puzzle Swap

thousandsofpieces.com ↗

A relative mentioned during dinner that she had boxes of old puzzles cluttering up her closet and wanted to give them a second life. We noodled on business models over dessert and had a landing page up before she got home. Thousands of Pieces is a community for puzzle lovers to swap finished puzzles with people who actually want them — matched to your taste, shipped with care.
