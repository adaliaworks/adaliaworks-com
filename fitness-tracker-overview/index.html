A simple fitness tracker built for the long game

A lean, web-based MVP for charting weightlifting goals and logging Zone 2 cardio — designed for adults pursuing the longevity-focused training principles popularized by Peter Attia and similar voices in the field.

Stage: Lean MVP · Two-User Pilot

Why we're building this

Most fitness apps are built for aesthetics or competition. This one is built around two specific, evidence-informed goals: hitting a consistent weekly training frequency, and progressively increasing strength over time — paired with the often-overlooked discipline of regular Zone 2 cardio. We're starting small (just two users — my wife and me), validating the core loop, and growing from there.

Strength, charted over time

Custom workout rotations (e.g., "Upper A / Lower / Upper B"), built around a simple four-set structure — warm-up plus three working sets — with weight and rep logging that drives a built-in progression model: when reps climb, weight goes up.

Cardio, made measurable

Zone 2 training is one of the simplest, highest-leverage longevity habits — and one of the easiest to skip. The app tracks duration, type, and time spent in Zone 2 or above, with weekly targets set during onboarding.

Dashboard at a glance

The home screen is built around momentum: a rolling 7-day view of training frequency, a one-tap entry into today's workout, and a quick read on cardio progress for the week.

Wireframe — dashboard home: rolling frequency counters, one-tap workout launch, expandable progress chart

A workout that adapts to a busy gym

Rather than forcing a strict order, each workout opens as a tappable list of exercises. Start whichever one is open — when a machine's in use, just move to the next. As each exercise is completed, it automatically drops to the bottom of the list, so what's left always rises to the top.

Wireframe — workout home: tap-to-start exercise list, auto-sorts completed items to the bottom

Progress you can actually see

A single, flexible chart pattern — choose your time range and your granularity — used consistently across the dashboard and individual exercises. Daily bars make rest days, vacations, and comeback stretches visible at a glance.

Wireframe — flexible volume chart with range & granularity selectors; sample data shows a steady climb, a two-week break, and a comeback

What's in the MVP

  • Custom workout rotations — user-defined splits (e.g., Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs) with a standard exercise library plus custom additions
  • Four-set logging structure — warm-up plus three working sets, pre-filled and editable, built around a double-progression strength model
  • Zone 2 cardio tracking — duration, activity type (with a growing shared preset list), and time spent in Zone 2 or above
  • Shareable progress — invite a friend (view-only) or a coach (suggest or directly adjust your rep/weight targets) via a simple shareable link
  • Smart, pace-aware reminders — SMS or email nudges that adapt to how close you are to hitting your weekly goals
  • "Workout Complete" summary — duration, exercise count, and a satisfying total-pounds-lifted number to close out every session
