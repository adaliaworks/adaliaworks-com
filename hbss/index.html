Each card is marked with where its answer came from. From the book — the printed contents, author's note, and cover. Verified online — the author's and publisher's own current pages. From the contents — the contents summaries rather than the chapter text, so it reflects the direction of the argument, not the author's exact wording. Not yet known — the Gaps deck: points the contents names but doesn't spell out. Reveal one, read the page it cites, and write the answer in. It becomes the back of that card from then on, and the card keeps its place in the rotation.
